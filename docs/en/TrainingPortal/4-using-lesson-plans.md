# Using training programs

Training programs allow trainers to organize which training modules students should complete.

There are two types of training programs:

- **Any order** – Participants can complete the selected modules in any order.
- **Lesson plan** – Participants must complete the modules in the order defined by the trainer.

Training programs are managed through the **Training Workx portal**.

You can access the portal at:

- Logistic Workx: portal.logisticworkx.com
- Cleaning Workx: portal.cleaningworkx.com

---

## Creating a training program

1. Log in to the **Training Workx portal**.
2. Go to **Training → Trainings**.
3. Click **+ Training program**.
4. Enter a name for the training program.
5. Choose the desired **Training form**:
   - **Any order** – Participants can complete the selected modules in any order.
   - **Lesson plan** – Participants must complete the modules in the predefined order.
6. Add the modules you want to include.
7. Save the training program.

## Assigning a training program

Once a training program has been created, it can be assigned to individual students.

1. Go to **Training → Students**.
2. Find the student and click **View modules**.
3. Click **Manage modules**.
4. In the top-right corner, use the **Training program** dropdown to select the training program you created.
5. Save the changes.

The selected training program is now assigned to the student.

---

## Using training programs in VR

When students start the VR training, they will see the modules from their assigned training program.

- If the training program is **Any order**, students can complete the modules in any order.
- If the training program is a **Lesson plan**, students must complete the modules in the predefined order.
